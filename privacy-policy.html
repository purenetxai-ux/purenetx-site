Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

The short version

PureNetX is built on a single principle — we never see your data. Our technology works on-device, generates safety decisions locally, and uploads nothing. This policy explains how that works in practice.

1. What we collect

When you interact with this website (purenetx.com), we may collect:

  • Email address — if you submit an inquiry or join our contact list
  • Basic usage data — page views and referral source, if analytics are enabled, with no personally identifiable information

That is the complete list.

2. What we never collect

This is the part that matters most:

  • We never collect images, video frames, or any visual data from a device camera
  • We never upload content from a user's device to our servers
  • We never log what content was intercepted or blocked
  • We never build a profile of what a user attempted to capture

Our context intelligence technology operates entirely locally. No data about camera activity, captured scenes, or blocked content ever leaves the device.

3. On-device processing

PureNetX's core technology runs a local AI model on the device itself. All context analysis happens in memory on the device in real time — no data leaves the device.

When a safety decision is generated, no record of that analysis is created, stored, or transmitted. Not locally. Not remotely.

4. Waitlist & contact data

If you submit your email address through this website, we store it securely for the sole purpose of contacting you about early access or design partnership opportunities. We will not:

  • Sell your email to any third party
  • Add you to unrelated marketing lists
  • Share your details without your explicit consent

You can request removal at any time by emailing purenetx.ai@gmail.com.

5. Cookies & analytics

This website uses minimal, privacy-respecting analytics only — no cross-site tracking cookies, no fingerprinting, no advertising pixels. If we integrate any analytics tool, it is configured to anonymise all data at the point of collection.

6. Third-party services

This website is hosted via Vercel. Contact form submissions are processed via Formspree. Both are GDPR-compliant services. We do not use social login, payment processing, or ad networks on this site.

7. Data retention

Contact email addresses are retained until you request deletion or we have concluded our outreach. We do not retain any other personal data beyond the session in which it was submitted.

8. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete any personal data we hold about you. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below. We will respond within 30 days.
