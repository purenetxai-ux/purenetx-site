Phase 3 — Research Direction

Integration  2028+

Long-term research vision. These represent where the technology needs to go — not what it does today.

These technologies are in research phase. Dates are aspirational. No OEM partnerships currently exist.

Camera System Integration

Long-term research direction

Research phase

Integrate the Context Intelligence Layer into live camera workflows, enabling privacy-preserving safety decisions before capture occurs. This requires camera API partnerships with device manufacturers — a non-trivial engineering and partnership challenge.

Camera-Level OS Integration

Long-term research direction

Partnership discussions

Integration into iOS, Android, and Samsung camera apps as a default safety layer. Context intelligence as a platform-level standard. Requires deep OEM and platform partnerships that do not yet exist.

Policy Engine

Long-term research direction

Designing

Customizable decision policies allowing platform operators to define their own safety thresholds and responses: Allow, Warn, Blur, Block, Escalate. Different platforms have different safety needs.

// Long-term vision — policy output
ALLOW    → Safe, consensual, in-context
WARN     → Risky — inform user
BLUR     → Reduce visibility
BLOCK    → Harmful content
ESCALATE → Report to platform trust & safety

OEM Partnerships

Strategic direction — long-term

Strategic planning

Integration into device cameras at the hardware level. Over two billion devices ship with cameras annually — reaching that installed base requires deep OEM partnership. This is the long-term TAM, not the near-term go-to-market.

Research Areas

What Pre-Capture Integration Requires

To achieve our long-term vision of integrating Context Intelligence directly into camera workflows, we are investigating a set of non-trivial research and engineering challenges.

None of these capabilities exist in today's prototype. Each represents a distinct research and partnership challenge requiring close collaboration with device manufacturers.

Research

Live Camera Stream Integration

Connecting the Context Intelligence Layer to real-time camera preview streams without impacting device performance.

Research

Edge AI Optimization

Model compression for real-time inference on constrained mobile and embedded hardware without cloud dependency.

Research

Camera HAL Integration

Hardware Abstraction Layer integration — requires direct collaboration with device manufacturers and OS vendors.

Research

iOS / Android Camera API

Working within Apple and Google's camera API constraints to embed decision logic at the capture layer.

Research

Shutter Interception

Mechanisms to act on contextual safety decisions before image capture completes — platform-level access required.

Research

OEM Camera Partnerships

Direct integration with Samsung, Apple, Google camera systems requires formal OEM partnership agreements.
