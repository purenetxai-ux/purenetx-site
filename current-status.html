Important Context — Current Limitation

The current prototype operates as an independent Context Intelligence Layer. It generates contextual safety decisions and policy recommendations that can be analyzed and applied by downstream systems. Direct integration into live camera systems for pre-capture intervention remains part of our long-term research and engineering roadmap.

The path to production pre-capture safety requires: camera API integration (iOS, Android, Samsung), real-time pipeline execution, shutter interception mechanisms, and OEM partnerships. These are under development and part of our Phase 2–3 roadmap.
