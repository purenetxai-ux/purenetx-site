About PurenetX

We're Building the Context Intelligence Layer for Cameras.

Not blocking content. Understanding it. Not punishing users. Protecting them. Not replacing judgment. Informing it.

Our Vision

PurenetX's long-term vision is to bring context-aware intelligence into camera systems, enabling privacy-preserving, on-device decisions that help prevent harmful visual content before it is created.

Mission

Why We Exist

Current systems are broken. Traditional AI moderation produces false positives across medical, educational, and family contexts. Binary decisions ignore human meaning entirely. Users lose trust in platforms that wrongfully block legitimate content. Better systems are possible — this is why we built PurenetX.

✕

The Problem

Traditional systems can produce false positives in medical, educational, and family contexts. Binary decisions ignore human context. Users lose trust when wrongfully blocked.

✓

The Solution We're Building

Context-aware decisions reduce false positives across diverse scenarios. Platforms retain creators. Safety improves without sacrificing utility. Users trust the system.

Values

What We Stand For

V.01

Privacy Is Non-Negotiable

We will never build a product that requires seeing user data. On-device processing is not a feature — it is a founding constraint.

V.02

Prevention Over Moderation

Content moderation is reactive. We work upstream — at the point of capture — where prevention is still possible.

V.03

Research-Driven Development

Built on established computer vision, AI safety, and privacy engineering principles. We validate before we claim.

V.04

Adversarial Thinking First

We test every bypass before shipping anything. We think like an attacker before we think like a builder.

V.05

Honest About Limitations

We acknowledge what we don't know. No unvalidated metrics. No hype. Transparent status at every stage.

V.06

Open to the Right Partners

We work with companies who take safety seriously. Design partnerships are collaborations, not sales.
