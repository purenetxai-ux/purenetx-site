About purenetX

Built on a simple
uncomfortable truth

Every camera-equipped device in the world — billions of them — captures first and asks questions later. The harm is already done the moment the shutter fires.

purenetX was founded to fix the only part of the pipeline that actually matters: the moment before capture. Not moderation. Not takedowns. Not reports. Prevention.

Adversarial thinking first
